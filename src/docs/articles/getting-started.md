KofaOS is where Kofa PG's work lives: who is doing what, how long it took, what went to the client, and what it cost. Everything hangs off one spine — an **account** has **projects**, a project has **tasks**, and time gets logged against those tasks. [How work is structured](/docs/how-work-is-structured) walks through that spine properly.

## Signing in

The app is at **kofaos.netlify.app**, and it is invite-only — the sign-in page says so: "Invite-only. Use your Kofa work email." If you have not been invited, an admin has to add you first; there is no way to create your own account.

Three ways in, all on the same screen:

- **Password** — your work email and password, then **Sign in**.
- **Email me a magic link** — a one-time link in your inbox, no password to remember. You will see "Magic link sent — check your inbox."
- **Continue with Google** — the fastest option if your Kofa address is a Google account.

![The sign-in screen](/docs/img/getting-started-sign-in.png)

> [!TIP] Use one method consistently
> All three land you in the same account. If you sign in with Google you never need to set a password at all — but you can set one later from [your profile](/docs/profile) if you want the option.

## Finding your way around

Three regions, and they do not move.

**The green bar across the top** carries the workspace name on the left (a menu, if you belong to more than one), and on the right the running **timer**, the **notification bell**, your name and photo — which is a link to your profile — and a sign-out button.

**The sidebar down the left** is your list of screens. It is not the same for everyone: KofaOS hides what your role cannot use rather than greying it out, so a colleague's sidebar may be longer than yours. [Roles and what each can see](/docs/roles-and-access) explains exactly why. **Help & docs** — these pages — is pinned at the bottom, with **Submit a ticket** below it for when the docs do not answer it.

**Everything else** is the screen you are on. Most screens have a title, a one-line description, and a **?** next to the title that brings you straight to the article about it.

![The main layout: green header, sidebar, and My work](/docs/img/getting-started-shell.png)

On a phone the sidebar collapses behind the menu button at the top left.

## Your first week

A reasonable order to get comfortable:

1. Open **[My work](/docs/my-work)**. This is your home screen and the one you will live in. Everything assigned to you is here, grouped by workstream.
2. Fill in **[your profile](/docs/profile)** — a photo and a job title make you much easier to find in the assignee and reviewer pickers everyone else uses.
3. Log time once, deliberately. Either start the **timer** in the header and stop it when you are done, or use **Log time** on your [timesheet](/docs/timesheet) to enter hours by hand. Do it once on purpose and the habit is set.
4. Skim **[Core concepts](/docs/how-work-is-structured)**. Ten minutes there saves a lot of confusion about why a task belongs to a workstream rather than a person.
5. Look at the **bell**. Notifications are how the app tells you a task landed on you, a deliverable needs your review, or a budget is running hot.

> [!NOTE] Nothing you do here is dramatic
> Almost everything in KofaOS is editable or reversible. Statuses can be changed back, archived things are archived rather than deleted, and the genuinely destructive actions all make you type the workspace name first. Click around.
