Your week of logged time, one screen. The subtitle carries the date range and the totals: how much you logged, and how much of it was billable.

## The week view

A grid: one row per project-and-task combination you worked on, seven day columns, and a **Total** column at the right. A **Daily total** row runs along the bottom.

Move between weeks with the arrows, or press **This week** to jump back.

Underneath, **Entries this week** lists every individual entry — task, project, date, note and hours — so you can see the shape of a day rather than just its sum.

![A week on the timesheet](/docs/img/timesheet-week.png)

Nothing logged yet reads: "No time logged this week." with the hint "Use the timer in the header, or log an entry manually."

## Two ways to log time

**The timer**, in the green header, is the accurate one — start it when you begin, stop when you finish. [My work](/docs/my-work) covers it.

**Log time** on this page is the manual one. It asks for:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Required |
| Task | Optional — appears once a project is chosen |
| Date | Defaults to today |
| Hours | Quarter-hour steps, minimum 0.25 |
| Note | Optional, but future-you will thank you |
| Billable | Ticked by default |

Time attaches to a project, and optionally to a task. Untick **Billable** for internal work that should not be charged to the client.

## What happens when a week ends

If you are a **contractor**, this is the part to understand.

Your week is submitted for approval **automatically** once it finishes. There is no submit button anywhere, and nothing for you to remember. From that moment the week travels a fixed chain:

**Open → With workstream lead → With managing director → Approved**, and then paid.

A week that is sent back reads **Sent back**, with the reason on it.

> [!WARNING] Submitted means locked
> The moment a week is submitted, its entries lock — before anyone has approved anything. Each entry shows a padlock: "This week is in approval — its time can't be changed."
>
> This is why logging as you go matters. Fixing a week after it has closed means someone sending it back to you.

If a week does come back, the card shows the reviewer's comment and a **Fixed — resubmit** button. Correct the entries, press it, and the week re-enters the chain at the start.

Employees do not go through this chain — only contractors do. The logging screen itself is identical for both.

> [!NOTE] "Paid" beats everything
> Once a week has been paid, its chip reads **Paid** whatever stage it was in before.

If you are the person on the other side of that chain, see [Timesheet approvals](/docs/timesheet-approvals).
