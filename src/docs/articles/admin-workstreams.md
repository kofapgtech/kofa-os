Workstreams are the company's teams — Studio, PPC, and so on. The page explains why they exist: "Company-wide teams. Tasks get routed to a Workstream instead of a project's roster; its lead assigns the work from there."

That routing is the point. Work arrives at a team rather than at a person, and the lead decides who picks it up. Nothing stalls because one individual is away.

Admins and executives.

## The list

**Name**, **Members**, **Lead** — leads listed by name, or `—` if nobody holds it.

![The workstreams list](/docs/img/admin-workstreams-list.png)

**New Workstream** asks for a name and nothing else. A new workspace starts with six already set up.

## Members and leads

Click a workstream to open it.

**Members** are the people who can be given hours on its tasks. Add someone with the **Add a person…** picker and **Add**; the icon beside a member removes them. Anyone can belong to more than one workstream.

The **Lead** pill next to a member makes them the workstream's lead, and clicking it again removes that. A lead confirms their people's timesheet weeks and staffs incoming tasks.

**Additional leads** covers the case where someone senior needs to lead a team without joining it: "An admin, executive, or HR can lead this Workstream alongside others, without moving them into it as a member." Add them with **Add a lead…**.

> [!NOTE] Membership is what unlocks hour allocation
> When you go to put someone on a task, only members of that task's workstream are offered. If a picker shows "No one in this workstream yet.", this page is where you fix it.

## Renaming and deleting

The pencil beside a workstream renames it. The bin deletes it, after a confirmation naming the workstream.

> [!WARNING] Deleting is not the same as emptying
> A workstream holds budget allocations and the tasks routed to it. Check what is pointing at one before removing it — renaming is almost always the better move if the team has simply changed name.

Admins, executives and HR can do all of this; it is the same permission that governs the page itself.

Leading more than one workstream is normal and supported; a person can lead several, and each has one entry in the Lead column.
