## Check here first

The search box at the top of the docs sidebar searches every article's full text, not just titles, so a phrase you can see on screen usually finds the right page. `Ctrl+K` (or `Cmd+K`) jumps to it from anywhere in the docs.

Every screen also has a **?** next to its title that opens the article about that screen.

## Is it broken, or is it your role?

Worth separating before you report anything, because the answer is usually the second one. KofaOS hides what your role cannot use rather than greying it out, so a missing screen is normally intended.

Three different things look similar:

1. **A screen missing from your sidebar** — your role does not include it.
2. **Figures showing `—` with a padlock** — you have the screen but not money access.
3. **Fewer rows than a colleague** — the records belong to another workstream or project.

[Roles and what each can see](/docs/roles-and-access) has the full table. If none of the three explains it, it is worth reporting.

## Reporting a problem

**Submit a ticket** — pinned at the foot of the sidebar, and linked at the end of every article. [Tickets](/docs/tickets) covers what happens next.

Include:

- **What you were doing** — the screen, and what you clicked.
- **What you expected**, and **what happened instead**.
- **Exact wording** of any error message.
- **When**, and **which workspace** if you belong to more than one.
- **A screenshot**, if the layout is part of the problem.

> [!TIP] The most useful sentence
> "On the Payment page, clicking Pay now for {person} shows 'Not approved yet' but their week shows Approved on my timesheet." — screen, action, expected, actual, and the exact words. That gets fixed. "Payroll is broken" gets a round of questions first.

## Something here is wrong or missing

These docs live in the codebase alongside the app, which means they can drift when a screen changes. If an article describes a button that is not there any more, that is worth reporting too — same route, and it takes a minute to fix.
