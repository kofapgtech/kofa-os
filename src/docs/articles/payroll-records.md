Every payroll payment recorded in this workspace, most recent first. The Payment page is where you act; this is where you look things up.

## The table

Four columns: **Employee**, **Pay period**, **Amount**, **Paid**.

Two filters above it, both starting at **All**: **Time period** and **Employee**. Between them you can answer most of the questions this page gets asked — what did we pay this person across the year, or what did a given period cost in total.

![Payroll records](/docs/img/payroll-records-table.png)

Click any row to reopen that payment's invoice: the per-project breakdown, hours and total exactly as it stood when it was recorded, with **Print invoice** if someone needs a copy.

## Empty states

"No payments recorded yet." with the hint "Payments show up here once they're paid from the Payment tab." — nothing has been paid.

"No payments match these filters." — your filters are too narrow. Set both back to **All**.

> [!NOTE] Records are the audit trail
> Nothing here is edited or deleted. If a payment was recorded wrongly, the correction is a conversation with whoever administers the workspace, not an edit on this screen — which is the point of keeping it immutable.
