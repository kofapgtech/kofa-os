KofaOS does not email you about everything. It puts what needs you behind the bell in the header.

## The bell

A count of unread notifications, up to "99+". Click it for the recent list, **Mark all read** to clear the badge, and **See all** for the full page. Nothing waiting reads "Nothing yet."

The full page at **/notifications** shows the same feed with unread items highlighted, and its subtitle keeps score: "{N} unread", or "All caught up".

![The notification list](/docs/img/notifications-bell.png?w=37)

## What you get told about

- **A task assigned to you** — you have been given hours on a task.
- **A task assigned to your workstream** — work has arrived at a team you lead, with nobody on it yet.
- **A time request** — someone has asked for more hours on a task, or your request has been decided.
- **A timesheet week** — a week has been submitted to your queue, or your own week has been approved or sent back.
- **A budget threshold** — a project has crossed its warning line.

## Clicking one

A notification is a link. Clicking it marks it read and takes you to the thing itself — the task, the project, the budget request — rather than to a list you then have to search. If you only ever use the bell as a to-do list, that is a perfectly good way to use it.

> [!TIP] Clear the bell, do not just silence it
> **Mark all read** clears the badge without opening anything, which is fine after a busy morning. But the two that reliably block other people are a **timesheet week in your queue** and a **deliverable waiting on you** — both also surface on [My work](/docs/my-work), so if you are unsure whether you missed one, look there.
