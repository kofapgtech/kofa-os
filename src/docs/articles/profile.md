Your own account. Reach it from your name in the top right, or from **/profile**.

## Your details

A photo, your name and title, and chips showing your role, workstream and employment type.

**Edit** makes the name and title editable; **Save** or **Cancel**. The camera button on your avatar changes your photo — it replaces the old one rather than keeping a history.

**Email** and **Capacity** are shown but not editable here. Email is fixed to the account; capacity is set by an admin on [the roster](/docs/admin-employees).

> [!TIP] Fill in the photo and title
> Not vanity. Your avatar is how people recognise you in assignee and reviewer pickers, in the approvals queue and on deliverable cards. A grid of grey initials slows everyone down.

![Your profile](/docs/img/profile-page.png)

## Your work at a glance

Three cards — **Hours this week**, **Open tasks** and **Overdue**, the last in red if there is anything in it. The same figures as the top of [My work](/docs/my-work), so you can check yourself without leaving the page.

## Account and security

Set or change a password: **New password**, **Confirm password**, then **Update password**. At least eight characters. You will be told if they are too short ("Use at least 8 characters.") or do not match ("Passwords don't match.").

Worth doing even if you always sign in with Google or a magic link — a password is a fallback for the day one of those is inconvenient.

**Sign out** is at the bottom, and also in the top right of every screen.
