The client list — "Your own work, your clients, and their read-only portal links."

> [!NOTE] Not everyone has this screen
> Contractors do not, whatever role they hold. Accounts is the commercial view of the workspace; contractors are staffed onto projects rather than onto the client relationship. The route is closed to them too, not just the link hidden.

## What a row shows

The account name, its contact name and email, and chips for anything notable — **Internal**, or **Closed**. If your role includes money you also get the account's hours and spend, and each of its projects as a small card with a status chip and a burn bar. Projects without a budget show **No budget · open-ended**.

Closed accounts are folded away behind a **Show {N} closed accounts** toggle.

![The accounts list](/docs/img/accounts-list.png)

## Statuses

**Prospect**, **Active**, **Paused**, **Closed**.

Closing an account archives its projects rather than deleting anything: "This closes the account and archives all its projects. Logged hours, invoices, and payment history are kept, and this can be undone by editing the statuses back."

## Creating one

**New account** — admins and executives only — asks for a **Name**, an optional **Code**, a **Status**, and a **Contact name** and **Contact email**. The contact is who the portal link is meant for, so it is worth filling in properly.

## The internal account

Every workspace keeps exactly one, pinned to the top, for the agency's own work. The app describes it directly: "This is the workspace's internal account. It can't be deleted — every workspace keeps exactly one, and it's where projects without a budget or an end date live."

Two things are true only of it: it has no client portal link, and its projects may run without a budget or an end date. Standing internal work — new business, admin, internal tooling — belongs here, so those hours are counted rather than lost.

## Portal links

On any client account, **Create client link** generates a read-only link the client can open without an account. After that the buttons become **Copy client link** and **Open**.

[The client portal](/docs/client-portal) covers what they see — and, more importantly, what they do not.
